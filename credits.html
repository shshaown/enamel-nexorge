NEXORGE / PORTFOLIO 001

Behind the experience.

Enamel is a fictional dental clinic and an original interactive portfolio concept for Nexorge. It is not an operating clinic. All treatment views are illustrative, not anatomical or diagnostic models.


Art direction

Inspired by the editorial typography and interactive-object approach of Vitreonique by Sajjad Hossen Shaown ↗. The user-supplied dental image informed the original procedural 3D jaw.


Photography

Dental room photograph by Ozkan Guner on Unsplash ↗. Used as illustrative environment imagery, not as a claim about a real Enamel location.


Technology

Original procedural WebGL geometry rendered with Three.js (MIT). Manrope and DM Sans via Google Fonts. No analytics, cookies, account integration, form submission, or persistent visitor storage.
